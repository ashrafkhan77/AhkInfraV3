What we do / SHEET 02

Full-scope steel detailing, start to fabrication-ready drawings.

From first model to final shop drawing, our team covers every stage of structural steel documentation.

Structural Steel Detailing

Connection-level detailing for beams, columns, and braces, checked against AISC and AWS standards before it ever reaches the shop floor.

3D Structural Modelling

Full building models in TEKLA and X-STEEL, coordinated across disciplines to catch clashes long before erection.

Shop & Erection Drawings

Fabrication-ready shop drawings and erection plans, formatted the way your shop floor and crew already work.

Connection Design

Moment and bearing connections, anchor bolt layouts, and embed detailing engineered for real-world tolerances.

Reinforced Concrete Design

Structural analysis and reinforced concrete detailing for hybrid steel-concrete buildings and pre-stressed elements.

Dedicated Engineering Teams
